I am a Master's student in Computer Science at **Queens College, City University of New York (CUNY)**, expected to graduate in June 2026. I received my B.Eng. in Electronic Engineering from South China Agricultural University, Zhujiang College.

My research sits at the intersection of **large language model (LLM) agents**, **coding theory**, and **AI for healthcare**. I am particularly interested in:

- **LLM agents for scientific workflows**: building trustworthy, privacy-preserving agentic systems that automate cohort-scale neuroimaging analysis.
- **LLMs meet information theory**: lossy text compression with LLM reconstruction, and when language models can help error-correcting codes for text.
- **Robust distributed learning**: coding-theoretic defenses against Byzantine attackers and stragglers in distributed training.

I work with Prof. Jun Li at Queens College and with Prof. Guorong Wu's group on agentic workbenches for neuroimaging.
